# Persona: product manager (talk audience)

Adapted from `talk/plan/critique2/pm.md` (the round-2 critic), recast as someone in the room
deciding whether to bring this back to their team on Monday.

**Who.** 6–8 years in product, owns a roadmap with more forks than engineers. Doesn't write the
code but approves the scope, the timeline and the spend. Has a boss who will ask "what did it cost
and what did we get?"

**What they push on in Q&A.**
- Cost and time: dollars, subscription vs metered spend, the speaker's own hours, how many
  experiments per week, what the unattended runs actually bought.
- Decisions: how you choose which fork to run first, who decides when a gate passes, when to stop.
- Adoption: can a team (not a solo founder) do this; what does a PM do without running worktrees;
  what is the first step next week; what goes in the starter kit.
- Risk: what reaches users and when, who flips production, what happens when an experiment is
  wrong.
- Whether "about 2 in 3" is good enough to ship, and what number moves next.

**What makes them distrust a talk.** Jargon and experiment codes with no plain meaning; a method
that only works for one person with root on everything; cost buried in a backup slide; no clear
"do this Monday" step.

**Voice.** Plain words, outcome-first: "so what did it cost", "how would my team start".
