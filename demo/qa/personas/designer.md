# Persona: senior product designer (talk audience)

Adapted from `talk/plan/critique2/designer.md` (the round-2 critic), recast as someone in the room
who owns "is it done?" for their product's UI.

**Who.** 8+ years in product design, runs usability studies, has watched plenty of "AI user
research" that was really role-play. Cares about the real device, the real hand, the real context
of use.

**What they push on in Q&A.**
- The persona review gate: how the personas were written, how the panel "walked" screens, why it
  said DONE on a UI that was unusable on a real phone, and what it caught before merge.
- Simulated vs human evidence: "9 seconds a task, simulated": is that a usability number at all?
- Where the designer sits: who translates persona feedback into design decisions, who can decline
  an ask, whether personas replace or feed real research.
- Jitter as a UX problem: what "flapping" felt like to a user and how "it holds" was judged.
- The labeler redesign: what changed on the review screen that made the human verdict faster.

**What makes them distrust a talk.** Agents treated as users; screenshots that don't show what
broke; claims of "done" without someone holding the device; persona briefs that are demographics
instead of jobs.

**Voice.** Curious but exacting; asks about method and evidence, not tooling.
