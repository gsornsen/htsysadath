# Story plan (storytelling discipline, 2026-09-24)

Audience: engineers, product managers, product designers. Built from the brief, the abstract,
decisions D4–D15 and the four persona critiques. Method borrowed from a prior project's story
canon (choose the shape the evidence actually has; make the twist structural; state it, don't
argue it; log what you moved and why). None of that project's content is used (D15).

## a) Pattern
- **Primary: situation → complication → resolution.** Push-through is the default (S); the cost
  of exploring collapsed (C, evidenced: ~100 experiments in two weeks within a flat subscription,
  D13); fan out, gate, decide fast (R). The complication is evidenced, not sized to the proposal.
- **Secondary: protagonist-arc** for the opening jitter story only. Real tension (testimony),
  real turning point (E19 against a control).
- **Tertiary: choreography** for the coordinator section and Demo 2 (lanes × time × handoffs),
  with Gerald visible as the decider at every merge.
- **Rejected as spine: what-is / what-could-be** (reads as a sales deck to this audience). Used
  only as a light close backed by a testable starter kit. **Kishōtenketsu** fits one beat: the
  remembered-vs-recorded twist.

## b) Three lenses
Engineer: mechanism, n/units/controls, stated uncertainty, visible failures. PM: the decision,
trade-offs, why now, cost, what got stopped. Designer: felt experience, the arc, method as craft.
Conflicts resolved by: one plain number per slide with stats in notes; separate visual grammar
for story vs evidence; takeaways split (persona drafts / gated lanes) and a shared starter kit.

## c) Beats (storyboard frames on SCR)
1 Character (S): the jitter, story · designer
2 Problem (S): 45 forks, one person, push through, story + framing · PM
3 Oh-crap (C): exploring got cheap, evidence · engineer, PM · Demo 1 kicks off
4 Solution (R): jitter resolved (E19: 35.8 → 15.4 → 0.14 per 100), evidence · engineer. Then gates
  (negative result was the win; what we stopped; four yardsticks; the lot bar) and the four acts
  as scope decisions · engineer, PM
5 Aha (R): the human stays the decider (panel as done gate and its failure; Demo 1 reveal +
  hybrid rule; two levers in labeling; coordinator + Demo 2), evidence with story at failure points
6 After (twist): remembered vs recorded, both directions · all · then habits, two actions, starter kit

## d) Honesty line
Evidence only: every number, every causal claim (both E19 levers), "held out" (D14), the lot bar
with its n. Testimony, labeled as Gerald's on screen: 3 min/label, "close to parking", the 3×3×5
grid, the felt jitter, the ~500 ms cadence. Visual grammar: evidence = chart + source footer;
story = words, quotation treatment, no numbers as data. Show the failures and counter-examples.
